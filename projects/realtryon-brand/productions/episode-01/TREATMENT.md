# Treatment

Twenty-three seconds, 1080×1920, 30fps. Warm white field `#F7F4F1`, plum ink `#160b14`, one red pill `#f44356`. Archivo Black for the claim, Inter for the rest. A quiet original three-note bed, no voice.

Five beats, each a full card:

1. Hook. "Try it on / while you move." The inset is the site's own live-mirror recording of a person turning in a striped shirt (`live-tryon-10.mp4`). A play button sits on it, then leaves.
2. Fitting room. "Camera off / until you start." The inset is a screenshot of /try-on: garment grid on the left, "Your live preview" with the camera off, the consent line, and "Start live mirror".
3. Glasses. "The look / moves with you." The inset is the site's live-mirror recording of sunglasses (`live-tryon-09.mp4`). The body says you can switch outfits in the same session, which the site states; the picture shows one look moving, not a fake cut between garments.
4. Photo Studio. "Then / compare." The inset is a screenshot of /photo-try-on. Copy is the page's own: saved live looks, or a photo beside an existing result. A note says sample people are reference images, not try-on results.
5. Close. "Open / Live Mirror", a "30s Free" pill, the full URL, and the fit disclaimer.
