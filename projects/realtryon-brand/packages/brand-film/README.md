# brand-film

The RealTryOn ad frame. `Episode` takes a timeline, two font stacks, two normalized live-mirror videos, and two product screenshots. Each `Beat` child is one card: its words, its hold, and which inset is on screen.

`inset` is `mirror`, `glasses`, `fitting`, `studio`, or `none`. The component does not draw a fake product. Copy lives in the SVML so the next episode edits words without editing this package.
