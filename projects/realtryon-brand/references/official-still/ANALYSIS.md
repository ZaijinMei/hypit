# Style reference

The supplied reference is the described cover of https://www.instagram.com/google/p/DG3Yp81x_Rh/. The post itself was not available as a video file in this session, so this note follows the written cover, not a frame-by-frame transcript.

What holds the cover: a clean white field, one large typographic claim, a few words picked out in colored rounded pills, a centered product inset (before/after or a split on a person) with a play button, and a pill CTA. It teaches one product idea per beat and does not look like chaotic UGC.

What this episode keeps: the white field, the pill, the inset, the play button, the sequence of short true feature beats, and a CTA that writes the URL out.

What it refuses: Google's logo, wordmark, and "Google Shopping" language. It also refuses a "3 ways" list, because RealTryOn is not three methods. The inset is RealTryOn's plum fitting room, not a redraw of that post's product.
